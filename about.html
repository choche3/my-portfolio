About Me

Computer Scientist.
Web Developer.
Founder.

I'm Joseph Mwale, a Computer Science student from The Copperbelt University in Zambia. I build websites and web applications — and I learn by doing.

This portfolio exists because I believe the best way to show what you can do is to actually do it. Every project here is real work: a real client, a real problem, or a real experiment.

I founded HPE as a way to turn my long childhood dream company into a business, using the skills i've acquired — delivering web solutions for people who need them. One of my recent projects was building the website for Realtime Fabricators, a fabrication company that needed a professional online presence.

Outside of code, I enjoy dancing, reading, and exploring ideas in physics and psychology. I think the most interesting problems sit at the intersection of fields — and I try to bring that curiosity into my work. Its always fascinated me with things that happen. How the mind works, anatomy of the human body, how the universe came to be, and how technology can be used to solve problems and make life better.

My goal is to become a full-stack web developer, building products that are both technically solid and genuinely useful. I'm early in that journey — and this portfolio is how I track the progress.
